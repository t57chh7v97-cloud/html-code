Nearby transit

Shows nearby subway stations and ferry terminals on the map and route card — useful for combining a ride with transit. Not routed by this app; it only shows what's nearby.

Bike parking

Shows bike parking/racks near your destination, so you know where to actually lock up.

Bike lanes

Highlights streets with a dedicated bike lane or path near your route, so you can eyeball how much of it is protected before you ride. Off by default — can be visually dense in built-up areas.

Surface warnings

Flags turn-by-turn steps that cross gravel, dirt, or other unpaved surfaces (marked below), based on map data — useful to know before you commit to a route on skinny tires.

Range and battery estimates are approximate — based on your assist mode's typical power draw plus climbing along the route, and current temperature where available. Actual range varies with rider weight, tires and wind.

Saved places

One-tap destinations shown on the search screen. Home and Work are built in — add more for school, the gym, a parent's house, anywhere you go often.
