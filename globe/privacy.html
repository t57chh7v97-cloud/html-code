Privacy Policy — Where & When

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Where & When is a live 3D globe that shows the time, daylight, weather and holidays anywhere on Earth. We collect as little as possible.

Without an account

Everything you do — your pin, settings, events, game scores, holiday progress — is saved only on your device. We don’t have servers that store it, and we don’t use ads, analytics or tracking.

Location

If you tap “My Location”, your device shares your location with the app so it can put your pin on the globe. To show live weather, routes and space-station passes, the app sends the pin’s coordinates (not your name or any ID) to these free public services: Open-Meteo (weather), OpenStreetMap routing (travel times), CelesTrak and wheretheiss.at (space station), NASA, NOAA and USGS (maps, aurora, earthquakes). Their own privacy policies apply.

Camera

The holiday photo booth uses the camera only while it’s open. Photos stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

If you create an account

Accounts are optional. If you sign up, we use Google Firebase to store your email address (or your Apple/Google sign-in ID), your nickname, and the settings and progress you choose to sync, so they appear on all your devices. We never sell this data or use it for advertising.

Deleting your data: open the app → 👤 Account → “Delete my account”. This permanently deletes your account and everything synced to it.

Shared features

Leaderboards, the Friendsgiving sign-up and other shared lists show the nickname and entries you choose to post to other people using the same shared page.

Children

The app has no ads and no tracking. Accounts are optional; younger users should ask a parent or guardian before creating one.
