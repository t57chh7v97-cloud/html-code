Where & When — Support

Thanks for using Where & When! Here are answers to common questions.

How do I find my town?

Type it in the search box at the top, or tap “My Location”.

Why don’t I see live weather or clouds?

Live data needs an internet connection. The globe, clocks and holidays still work offline.

How do I delete my account?

Open 👤 Account and choose “Delete my account”.

Contact

Report a problem or send an idea with the 💬 Feedback button in the app (⋯ More → Feedback), or open an issue on GitHub.
