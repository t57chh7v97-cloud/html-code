Add to phone or tablet (no app store needed)
iOS (iPhone/iPad):
1. Open FlightFlow in Safari
2. Tap the Share button (box with arrow)
3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Name it FlightFlow and tap Add

Android:
1. Open FlightFlow in Chrome
2. Tap the 3-dot menu
3. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"
4. It installs like a real app!
For real App Store / Google Play
To publish on the App Store or Google Play, you need to wrap this HTML file in a native shell:

• Capacitor (free) — convert any web app to iOS/Android. capacitorjs.com
• Cordova (free) — older but widely supported. cordova.apache.org
• PWABuilder (free) — Microsoft tool to package PWAs. pwabuilder.com

App Store requires Apple Developer account ($99/yr). Google Play is $25 one-time.
